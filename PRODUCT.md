# ICare MC Admin

## Register

product

## Product

Internal admin for ICare MC: mothers, doctors, appointments, content, and finance ops connected to `api.icaremchealth.com` (staging) or Supabase (legacy).

## Users

Clinic/ops admins and super-admins who monitor platform health, verify doctors, manage bookings, and review payments.

## Brand personality

Calm clinical green, dense and task-first — Stripe/Linear familiarity over marketing flair.

## Anti-references

No purple-on-white AI defaults, no hero marketing layouts, no decorative dashboard chrome.

## Design principles

1. Data before decoration — KPIs and tables first.
2. One accent (emerald) for primary actions and selection.
3. Same StatCard / admin-panel vocabulary across screens.
4. Prefer clear empty and loading states over spinners in content.
